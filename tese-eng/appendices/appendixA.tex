\chapter{Reproducibility}
\label{ap:reprodutibilidade}

This appendix documents the provenance of the results presented in the body of the dissertation. It
lists the artefacts kept, establishes the correspondence between each result reported and the
procedure that produces it, presents the state in which each substantive claim was left after being
measured, and records the licensing conditions of the data used. It closes with the design of the
study with users that Section~\ref{sec:con_limitacoes} identifies as the gap of greatest weight in
the work.

\section{Artefacts kept}
\label{sec:ap_artefactos}

This section lists what is kept with the work and what is not, with the reason in each case. The
code, the results files and the model artefacts described here accompany the dissertation and can
be made available to the committee; this appendix exists so that the provenance of every value is
verifiable from this document, without depending on that access.

The execution environment is described in Section~\ref{sec:met_ferramentas}, with the exact
versions of the libraries that produced the values in this document. The versions are pinned, and
not expressed as ranges, because a library that updates itself changes results without issuing a
warning. None of the procedures requires a graphics processing unit.

Three classes of artefact are kept. The first is the learned component, comprising the trained
model, the calibrator fitted on the validation block and a descriptor file of the run that produced
them, with the size of each block, the prevalence of positive cases, the seed used and the metrics
obtained. The second is the set of results files, one per evaluation procedure, that the text
cites. The third is the precedent base the deployed application consults, with fifty-six megabytes
of vectors and eight of metadata. This last one is not a sample: it is the artefact the system needs
in order to start, and without it there would be no precedents to present.

The source corpora are not kept. The historical set occupies several hundred megabytes and is
rebuilt by the preparation procedures described in Section~\ref{sec:met_dados}. Reduced samples are
kept in their place, whose purpose is to let the automatic verification run without network access,
including the test that enforces the temporal separation described in
Section~\ref{sec:met_triagem} and that Listing~\ref{lst:lookahead} reproduces.

\section{Origin of each result}
\label{sec:ap_numeros}

This section establishes the correspondence between each result reported in the body of the
document and the procedure that generates it.

No result in this dissertation was entered manually into the text. Each one is produced by an
automatic procedure with a fixed seed, which writes its own results file, and it is that file the
text cites. A second run over the same data returns the same values. Table~\ref{tab:ap_numeros}
brings together the results reported and identifies the section in which each one appears.

The verification this chain permits is possible from this document, and that is why the appendix
describes it rather than delegating it. The section given in the third column states, for each
result, the protocol, the baseline against which it was compared and the population it covered; and
Section~\ref{sec:met_ferramentas} pins the exact library versions and the seed. A reader who has
the source data, whose provenance and licensing are given in Section~\ref{sec:ap_dados}, has what
is needed to rebuild any of the measurements without recourse to this work.

The determinism guarantee applies to the upper block of the table. The rows of the lower block are
of another nature, and are therefore separated: they are measurements over the operation of the
system, whose value depends on the moment at which the log was read and not on a seed. The section
in which each one is presented declares the period and the set over which it was carried out.

One of those measurements is not regenerable, and the distinction is recorded rather than omitted.
The breakdown of the funnel by gate, presented in Section~\ref{sec:sis_politica}, was read from the
decision log on a concrete day, and that log keeps only three days, for the reason given in
Section~\ref{sec:met_dados}: it is republished at every cycle, so the cost is one of publication and
not of storage. Running the same procedure on a later date produces another day, and not the same
one. The value was kept together with the date of the reading and the identification of the
procedure that produced it, so that any future reading has a declared origin even though that day
does not recur.

\section{Data, licences and attribution}
\label{sec:ap_dados}

This section records the origin of the data used and the obligations their licensing imposes.

The historical set that underpins the precedent base is \gls{FNSPID}
\autocite{dong2024fnspid}, used under the Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0 licence, whose attribution is
fulfilled here. Only minimal excerpts of headlines from the corpus appear in the document, for
illustration.

That licence carries a second obligation, which is triggered in this case. Since files derived
from \gls{FNSPID} are distributed, the share-alike clause applies to those files. The
licence of the code is a decision that remains open in this document, and this is the constraint
that conditions it.

Prices come from free market data services, through the chain of alternatives described in
Section~\ref{sec:sis_dados}; real-time news comes from three free interfaces, within their
respective usage limits, compared in Section~\ref{sec:sis_fontes}.

As to personal data, the exact formulation appears in Section~\ref{sec:met_privacidade}: the
system does not know the users' positions and does not ask for them, and the public channel does
not identify recipients. The exception is the optional subscription to personalised alerts, in
which the conversation identifier assigned by the platform and the list of companies chosen are
associated, and those are the only elements stored.

\subsection*{Cost of operation, and the reason for the tier chosen}

The system ran for most of the documented period in containers of the lowest tier, at a cost of
seven dollars per month per process. On 4 September 2026 the collection process began persistently
exceeding the memory quota of $512$\,MB, with over-quota warnings at a frequency of approximately
two per minute and readings between $518$ and $970$\,MB.

Four causes were measured and corrected: the live case base was loaded into Python \emph{float}
lists, at a cost of $136.7$\,MB for $10{,}968$ records, against $21.7$\,MB for the same information
in a single-precision matrix; the bases and the encoder were rebuilt at every sixty-second cycle;
the file of pending cases was read once per company; and publishing a $40$\,MB file produced five
copies of it in memory. Together they reduced the peak from $970$ to $663$\,MB.

\textbf{The corrections were not enough, and that is the honest result.} The working set of the
application genuinely exceeds a $512$\,MB container: the numerical libraries and the inference
engine occupy, on their own, $158$\,MB, and the precedent matrix becomes resident as soon as the
first query walks its pages. The process settled at $536$\,MB even between peaks.

The technical alternative would be to remove the base of $38{,}214$ precedents from the process,
which would degrade the quality of retrieval, that is, would trade an infrastructure limitation for
a limitation of result. The option taken was the reverse, and the cost appears in
Table~\ref{tab:ap_custo}.

\section{Design of the study with users}
\label{sec:ap_estudo}

This section presents the design of the study referred to in Section~\ref{sec:con_futuro}.

The study compares two conditions over six real alerts taken from the channel. Condition A presents
the fact without an explanation and condition B presents the complete alert. Two of the six cases
are situations in which the topic of the news and the direction of the movement disagree, a
configuration in which Section~\ref{sec:av_qi2} shows the system to be more liable to incorrect
interpretation.

The counterbalancing crosses two factors, the order of the conditions and which half of the stimuli
serves as condition A. The second factor is necessary because the two conditions use distinct
alerts, on pain of the second measuring memory instead of comprehension; without the crossing, any
difference between conditions would be confounded with the greater accessibility of one of the
halves.

The central question is the one the automatic check does not reach. The correspondence between each
sentence of the alert and the fact that supports it is guaranteed by construction, but the claim of
the product describes a path travelled by a reader: given a sentence with a reference, to reach the
corresponding fact and judge autonomously whether it supports the sentence. The question is
qualitative and does not require a large sample.

Two methodological safeguards are fixed in the code before any data exist: the threshold of eight
participants for the statistical test, whose alteration would be recorded in the version history,
and the analysis procedure, which over the unfilled sheet produces no result at all. A third block
of the original design, concerning generated text, is not executable, since the routes that served
it were removed from the system.
