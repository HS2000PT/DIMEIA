\chapter{Correspondence with the curriculum}
\label{ap:curriculo}

This appendix establishes the correspondence between the course units of the master's degree and the
points of the work at which their subject matter was applied. Table~\ref{tab:ap_curriculo} presents
that correspondence and also identifies the units whose subject matter found no application, since a
correspondence that finds an application for every unit at every point conveys no information.

Two entries of the table declare an absence of application: the subject matter of Intelligent
Environments and the logic and constraint programming component. Recording them is more informative
than constructing an artificial link.
